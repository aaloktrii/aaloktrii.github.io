About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Physics at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Jyoti Katoch in the Lab for Investigating Quantum Materials, Interfaces and Devices (LIQUID). My research is driven by a focus on the emergent phenomena within low-dimensional and strongly correlated systems, and my experimental work is broadly categorized into two main directions.

First, I aim to uncover the fundamental electronic interactions of strongly correlated systems. To do this, I primarily utilize angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) to directly visualize their momentum-resolved, many-body interactions. Concurrently, I investigate 2D magnetism by employing X-ray magnetic circular dichroism photoemission electron microscopy (XMCD-PEEM) to spatially map the nanoscale magnetic order and domain structures of atomically thin quantum materials and spintronic devices.

I regularly conduct experiments at leading international synchrotron facilities, including the Advanced Light Source (ALS) (USA), NSLS-II (USA), BESSY II (Germany), Synchrotron SOLEIL (France), and ALBA (Spain).

My approach to these experiments is highly comprehensive, bridging nanoscale device fabrication, advanced spectroscopy, and the statistical analysis of high-dimensional datasets. I fabricate complex van der Waals heterostructures and microelectronic devices from the ground up, utilizing cleanroom nanofabrication techniques and controlled glovebox environments. Before taking these systems to the beamline, I characterize their structural and electronic properties in-house using atomic force microscopy (AFM), Raman spectroscopy, and preliminary transport measurements. Finally, to extract meaningful physics from the massive, multidimensional datasets generated during beamtimes, I pair my experimental methodology with robust statistical modeling and machine-learning-based analysis.

My foundation in condensed matter physics began at IISER Kolkata, India, where I earned an integrated BS–MS in Physics. I conducted research at the National Centre of High Pressure Studies under Prof. Goutam Dev Mukherjee, and in the Advanced Functional Materials Lab with Prof. Sayan Bhattacharyya. During this time, my work focused on probing structural and vibrational properties through low-temperature and high-pressure Raman spectroscopy and four-probe transport measurements, in addition to the synthesis of novel polycrystalline condensed matter systems.
