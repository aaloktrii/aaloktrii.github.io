About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Physics at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Jyoti Katoch in the Katoch–Singh Lab. My research uses nano/micro-angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (nano-ARPES) and X-ray magnetic circular dichroism photoemission electron microscopy (XMCD-PEEM) to directly visualize the momentum-resolved band structure and nanoscale magnetic order of atomically thin quantum materials. I carry out these experiments at international synchrotron facilities including the Advanced Light Source (USA), NSLS-II (USA), BESSY II (Germany), Synchrotron SOLEIL (France), and ALBA (Spain).

Alongside spectroscopy, I fabricate van der Waals devices in the clean room and synthesize quantum and energy materials, pairing hands-on experimental work with statistical modeling and machine-learning-based analysis. Before Carnegie Mellon, I completed an integrated BS–MS in Physics at IISER Kolkata, India.
