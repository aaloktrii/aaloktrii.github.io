About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Physics at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Jyoti Katoch in the Lab for Investigating Quantum Materials, Interfaces and Devices (LIQUID). My current research focus lies within low dimensional, correlated systems.

I primarily use spatially resolved angle resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) to directly visualize their momentum resolved spectral function. Additionally, employing X-ray magnetic dichroism photoemission electron microscopy (XPEEM), i study emergant magnetic pheneomenon in 2D limit. I perform my experiments at leading international synchrotron facilities, including the Advanced Light Source (ALS) (USA), BESSY II (Germany), Synchrotron SOLEIL (France), ALBA (Spain), and NSLS-II (USA). I extract meaningful physics from the multidimensional datasets generated during beamtimes using a combination of statistical modelling and machine learning methods.

I utilize cleanroom and controlled glovebox environment for nanofabricating van der Waals heterostructures and microelectronic devices. I also utiloize in house atomic force microscopy (AFM), Raman spectroscopy, and preliminary transport setup for structural and electronic characterization. Before taking these systems to the beamline, I characterize their structural and electronic properties in-house using atomic force microscopy (AFM), Raman spectroscopy, and preliminary transport measurements. Finally, to extract meaningful physics from the massive, multidimensional datasets generated during beamtimes, I pair my experimental methodology with robust statistical modeling and machine-learning-based analysis.

My foundation in condensed matter physics began at IISER Kolkata, India, where I earned an integrated BS–MS in Physics. I conducted research at the National Centre of High Pressure Studies under Prof. Goutam Dev Mukherjee, and in the Advanced Functional Materials Lab with Prof. Sayan Bhattacharyya. During this time, my work focused on probing structural and vibrational properties through low-temperature and high-pressure Raman spectroscopy and four-probe transport measurements, in addition to the synthesis of novel polycrystalline condensed matter systems.
